Sections are numbered automatically on the public page. Use the arrows to reorder and the remove control to delete a section.

How to format section text

  • Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
  • Start a line with - to create a bullet list.
  • Put **two asterisks** around words to make them bold.
  • Write a link as [link text](/contact/).
  • Tick Show as a highlighted contact panel on a section to add a highlighted panel around it.
