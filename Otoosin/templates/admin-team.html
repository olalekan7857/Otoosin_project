Invite a staff member

The account stays inactive until the recipient opens the invitation link and sets a password. No password is included in the email.

What each role can access

  • OwnerFull access: team, catalogue, inventory, delivery, settings and enquiries.
  • ManagerCatalogue, inventory, delivery, business settings and enquiries. Cannot manage the team.
  • SupportEnquiries only: read, update status and add internal notes.
  • FulfilmentOrders only. Reserved until order management is added.
