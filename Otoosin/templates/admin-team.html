  4. Sign inForgotten passwords can be reset from the team sign-in page.

Invite a team member

The account stays inactive until the recipient opens the invitation email and sets a password. The email never contains a password.

What each role can access

  • OwnerFull access: team, catalogue, inventory, delivery, settings and enquiries.
  • ManagerCatalogue, inventory, delivery, settings and enquiries. Cannot manage the team.
  • SupportEnquiries only: read, update status and add internal notes.
  • FulfilmentOrders only: view orders and update the permitted fulfilment statuses.
