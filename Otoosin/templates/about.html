About O’TOOSIN Farms

A complete farm shop for everyday food

O’TOOSIN Farms brings meats, fish, live animals, eggs, veggies and fruits together in one place. Browse by category, choose what you need, and select delivery or pickup at checkout.

Who we are

One storefront for the whole farm range

O’TOOSIN Farms is built around a simple idea: the foods a household needs should be easy to find in one place. Instead of separating meat from produce, or live animals from fish, the shop brings the full farm range together.

Our slogan — “Delivering safe, healthy, and nutritious foods at your doorstep . . .” — reflects what we work towards with every order. Each listing brings the category, unit, price and available product state together, so you can make an informed choice before adding anything to your cart.

Whether you are shopping for a family meal, a celebration or the week ahead, you can browse the categories, build a single cart and choose how you want to receive your order.

Getting your order

From checkout to your doorstep — or a pickup location

Choose delivery to a supported location or collect your order from a pickup location. Your order total updates based on your selection. Delivery includes doorstep delivery in Abeokuta and park delivery to Lagos, Ibadan and Ijebu, subject to approved arrangements.

Delivery

Doorstep delivery is available in Abeokuta. Park delivery serves selected destinations, including Lagos, Ibadan and Ijebu, subject to approved arrangements. The applicable delivery fee is included at checkout based on your selected location.

Pickup

Choose a pickup location and collect your order yourself. No delivery fee applies.

Complete your order and payment securely at checkout. Your total includes your selected products and any applicable delivery fee.
