Create main categories and subcategories, edit their details, and control what customers can browse.

How categories work

  1. Main category: a broad group customers browse first, such as Meats, Fish or Eggs. Leave the parent as “Main category”.
  2. Subcategory: a specific group inside a main category, such as Pork, Chicken or Beef. Choose the main category as its parent.
  3. Products: assign every product to the exact subcategory it belongs to. A main category is only a container.
