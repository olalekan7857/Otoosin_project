How ordering works

Four steps from browsing to delivery or pickup

01

Browse the farm shop

Choose a product category, then open the item you need or browse the related range.

02

Choose quantity and unit

Set the quantity shown for that product and add it to your cart.

03

Review your order

Enter your contact and delivery details, choose how to receive the order, and review the total before finishing.

04

Complete your order
