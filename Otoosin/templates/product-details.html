Before you order

Products, units and delivery, clear from the start

Every O’TOOSIN Farms listing shows the product type, its condition and the price basis. Nothing is added to your cart until you choose the quantity.

Clear unit pricing

Each listing shows whether it is sold by kilogram, crate, basket or as a live chicken. Adjust the quantity and the total updates before you add it to your cart.

Condition stated

Fresh, frozen, fried and live items are labelled on every listing, so you know what you are ordering before it reaches your kitchen.

Delivery at checkout

Delivery fees are shown at checkout based on your location. You can also choose pickup at the selected pickup location.

Order when you are ready

Add what you need to the cart, review your order and check out with your delivery details. Nothing is prepared until you place the order.
