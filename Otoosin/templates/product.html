Ordering guide

Know exactly what you are ordering

Every product card follows the same clear layout, from the product category and its condition to the price unit and the cart action.

  • Category and product — the exact item and farm category you are ordering.
  • Condition where it applies — fresh, frozen or fried for meats and fish; live for animals.
  • A clear price unit — per kilogram, crate or basket. Add to your cart, then adjust the quantity there.
